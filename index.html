My name is Tetiana, and I am a motion designer from Ukraine. I specialize in motion design, motion art, 3D graphics, stop motion, video mapping, and stage design. I have had the pleasure of working with clients such as Microsoft, Uber, Leopold Museum Vienna, Readdle, and others.


I have always been the creative one among technicians. Even now, to bring my projects to life, I rely on others to build, construct, or code them. Currently, I am leading an art project called "The Treasure" — a series of miniatures dedicated to the destroyed heritage in Ukraine.
